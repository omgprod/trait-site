Data & transparency

Privacy
policy.

Updated: 25 October 2026 · Applies to the current version of Trait.

♥
No account. No ads. No data collected by the game.

Your progress stays on your device.

1. Who publishes the game?

Trait is published by Babapp. For questions about this policy or your data, email contact@babapp.fr.

2. Accounts and personal data

Trait does not require an account, contain advertising or use analytics or advertising tracking tools. The game does not send personal data to its publisher.

3. Local save data

The game stores progress on your device, including level stars, unlocked purchases and equipment choices. This data is not sent to Babapp. Deleting the app or resetting your device may erase it.

4. Freemium and payments

The first three levels are free. Unlocking the full game and optional companions are offered as one-time in-app purchases, with no subscription. On iOS, transactions are processed by the App Store. Apple’s displayed price and availability apply.

Babapp does not receive your payment card details. Apple processes payments under its own privacy policy. Trait stores purchase entitlements locally to unlock acquired content and support restoration.

5. Children

The game does not collect personal data and includes no chat or player-published content.

6. Changes to this policy

This page will be updated if the game’s features or data practices change. The date above shows the latest update.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, email contact@babapp.fr. Since the game does not send saves to the publisher, Babapp has no copy to access or delete.

About this website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses and technical visit information for hosting security and operation under its own privacy policy. The Trait website does not add analytics or advertising.
