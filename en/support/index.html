Frequently asked questions

Before you draw

Which levels are free?

The first three levels are free and introduce drawing, the ink limit and shelter physics.

How do I unlock the full game?

The full game and some companions are available as one-time purchases, with no subscription. On iOS, payment is handled by the App Store; Apple’s displayed price and availability apply.

Do companions give me an advantage?

No. Companions change appearance only, not physics, ink or difficulty.

Do I need an account?

No account is needed. Stars, purchases and preferences are saved on your device.

Does the game show ads?

No. Trait contains no advertising.

Are stars a currency?

No. Stars track your results and unlock some cosmetic rewards. They are never spent.

Where is my save data?

Progress is stored locally. Deleting the app or resetting your device may erase the save.

Questions about your data? Read the privacy policy or email contact@babapp.fr.
